---
title: Private numeric limits
description: Prove that a payment satisfies hidden numeric thresholds without publishing the thresholds.
---

The payment policy contains two private numbers:

```text
maxPaymentAmount
paymentApprovalThreshold
```

A transfer must satisfy the hard maximum. If it crosses the approval threshold, a trusted approval must also be present.

```text
amount <= maxPaymentAmount
AND
(amount <= paymentApprovalThreshold OR approved == true)
```

The demo policy uses a £5,000 hard maximum and a £4,000 approval threshold. Those numbers are visible to the local policy owner in the playground, but they are not written to the public Midnight ledger.

An £8,000 request remains denied even when approval is present because approval cannot override the hard maximum.
