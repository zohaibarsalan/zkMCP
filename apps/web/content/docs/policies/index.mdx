description: The authorization constraints implemented by the current Compact contract.
---

The hackathon contract intentionally supports a small set of deterministic policy primitives rather than a general policy language.

Implemented primitives:

- resource membership
- tool permission
- private numeric maximum
- private approval threshold
- trusted human approval
- agent identity
- replay protection

These primitives are enough to demonstrate documents, email, and payments without pretending the prototype already has a full DSL.
